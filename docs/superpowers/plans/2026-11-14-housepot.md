# Housepot Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A phone-first web app where housemates pay a deposit into a house pot, chores rotate, and skipping or missing a chore moves real money from your balance to whoever does it with photo proof.

**Architecture:** One Next.js app. All rules live in `src/domain/*` as plain functions that take a `Db` and a `now`, so they are tested against real Postgres without the web layer. Money is a double-entry ledger; balances are always derived. Payments, texts and photo storage sit behind interfaces with fake versions. A Kubernetes CronJob calls an internal tick endpoint that assigns turns, reminds, and charges misses.

**Tech Stack:** Node 20, pnpm, Next.js 15 (App Router, `output: "standalone"`), TypeScript, `pg`, plain SQL migrations, luxon, vitest, Biome, Stripe SDK, Twilio over `fetch`, `@aws-sdk/client-s3`, Kubernetes with Kustomize, kind for the local cluster, GitHub Actions.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-housepot-design.md`

Never push to any other account or organisation.
- Frontend quality (owner's request): it must look clean and deliberate, not like a generated template. One small set of design tokens (colour, type scale, spacing, radius) in `globals.css`, system font stack, no gradients, no emoji as icons beyond the chore glyph, no card-inside-card nesting, consistent 44px minimum tap targets, light and dark themes. Follow the frontend design guidance found on disk during execution.
- Money is integer cents everywhere. Currency CAD.
- Defaults from the spec: deposit 2000, skip price 500, dispute window 24 hours, time zone `America/Toronto`.
- Card fee shown on top: `feeCents(a) = ceil((a + 30) / (1 - 0.029)) - a` (91 for 2000).
- Texts are only: morning-of reminder, two-hours-before reminder, bounty opened, balance changed by a miss or dispute, monthly top-up request, sign-in code.
- The app must run and all tests must pass with `SMS_PROVIDER=fake PAYMENTS_PROVIDER=fake STORAGE_PROVIDER=disk` and no outside accounts.
- Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, at most 8 MB.
- One active house per person.

## Review Focus

1. Two housemates claim the same open bounty at the same moment: exactly one is paid. (Task 6)
2. The payment provider delivers the same success event twice: the member is credited once. (Task 4)
3. The tick runs twice at a deadline, or two ticks overlap: the miss is charged once and each text is sent once. (Task 8)
4. A chore due at 21:00 stays at 21:00 local time across the March and November clock changes. (Task 5)
5. Rotation with members who are pending or have left, and a house with no active members: non-active members are never assigned; no active members means no turn is created. (Task 5)

### Task 8: The tick

**Files:** Create `src/domain/tick.ts`, `test/tick.test.ts`.

**Interfaces — Produces:**
```ts
export async function runTick(db, p: { sms: Sms; payments: Payments }, now: Date, baseUrl: string):
  Promise<{ turnsCreated: number; reminders: number; misses: number; topupRequests: number; autoReloads: number; settled: number; skippedLocked?: true }>;
```
One tick, under `pg_try_advisory_lock` (returns `skippedLocked` when another tick holds it), for every unpaused house:
1. `missTurn` each `assigned` turn past its deadline; text the assignee their new balance and text the other active members that the bounty is open.
2. `ensureTurns`.
3. Morning reminder: once per turn, when local time is 08:00 or later on the due date and the turn is still `assigned`. Final reminder: once, when within two hours of the deadline. Each is claimed with `UPDATE turns SET reminded_..._at = now WHERE id = $1 AND reminded_..._at IS NULL` before sending.
4. Auto-reload: active members with `auto_reload`, a `customer_ref`, and balance at or below 0 are charged `deposit - balance` with idempotency key `reload:<memberId>:<latest transfer id>`; the returned event goes through `applyPaymentEvent`.
5. Monthly top-up request: on or after the 1st, members below the deposit whose `topup_requested_month` is not this month get one text and the month is recorded.
6. Settlement: houses whose `settlement_date` is today or earlier are settled (Task 9's `settleHouse`).
